Situation families: mockups

  • One Board for every Situation. Three rows (Lead · Approach · Support · Successes; the Support box is open, for any number of pawns), a Progress track (0–8), a Round track (1–6) and the Tension box. A Situation that doesn’t use a track leaves it empty.
  • The Situation card says how to set the Board up (its Setup box), and how it’s won and lost.
  • Every success still earns 1 spotlight: from the row’s stack when the stacks are what you’re clearing, otherwise from the scene pool, a fixed pile set out at setup (6 in the Beginning, 9 after), so every scene is worth about the same.
  • Three generic shape tokens, blank on one side and ● ▲ ■ on the other, for anything hidden. The card says what each shape means this time.
  • A mockup only: none of these rules are in the game yet. Green underlined words are blanks filled from the Element cards; ? tiles are the face-down Risks.
